# User-approved Contact page freeze

The public Contact page is approved and frozen. Do not change its design, layout,
spacing, typography, map, opening hours, responsive behavior or form structure
unless the user explicitly requests it. This includes the Contact page route and
`components/concepts/contact/ConceptContactPage.tsx` and `ContactPage.module.css`,
and effects from shared styles/components.

Server-side email routing is documented in `docs/PUBLIC_FORM_EMAIL_ROUTING.md`.
Contact messages go to `info@ellstorpskrog.se`; reservation notifications go to
`reservation@ellstorpskrog.se`. Keep recipients separate and secrets server-side.
